## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Predeclared falsifiable composition metrics

The lab SHALL fix metrics and bounded engineering targets before G1. It SHALL
measure actually painted geography, not an empty viewport, distinguish pixel
luminance from display brightness and power, and reject flat bright rectangles,
missing text, detached markers and map obstruction. No aggregate score may
compensate a failed hard constraint.

#### Scenario: Discriminate misleading brightness
- **WHEN** a candidate is measured
- **THEN** visible map area, mean linear luminance, land/water distinction, retained area and connectivity are recorded
- **AND** sufficient brightness alone cannot accept a blank or occluded map

### Requirement: Preserved information and deliberate alignment

Compositions SHALL retain exact time, four selected fields, rocket/model,
operator, purpose, site, countdown and separate status indicators. They SHALL
retain previous text minima, projected geographic identity and a full ring marker.
Full text, circle fit, foreground separation, actual background contrast and
declared text axes SHALL be checked on short and long RU/EN scenes.

#### Scenario: Read a complete face
- **WHEN** a scene is rendered
- **THEN** every required value is retained without clipping, accidental overlap or an invented coordinate
- **AND** metrics expose alignment residuals and any failed contrast or spacing constraint

### Requirement: Adaptive generations against exact controls

Research SHALL execute18,12,6 new parameter sets, with six initial spatial
hypotheses and traceable, observed parent selection. Both previous five layout
finalists and current five marker finalists SHALL remain exact controls.

#### Scenario: Evolve a measured hypothesis
- **WHEN** a generation is completed
- **THEN** actual observations and explicit rejection reasons inform the next generation
- **AND** duplicate parameters or unknown parents are rejected and failures retained

### Requirement: Whole composition review and bounded conclusions

Final selection SHALL compare up to three proposals with exact controls on equal
data and scales, freeze parameters before six additional scenes and preserve any
failure. Numerical proxies SHALL be supplemented by actual browser image review
of hierarchy, grouping, figure-ground continuity and balance. Self review SHALL
not be presented as independent or human/device evidence.

#### Scenario: Inspect final proposals
- **WHEN** a final proposal is recommended
- **THEN** its measurements, actual browser image and limitations are linked
- **AND** unresolved material visual defects or additional-scene failures prevent a claim of acceptance

### Requirement: Durable research and publication boundary

The objective, metric versions, feature hypotheses, progress, sources, generations,
observations and decisions SHALL remain in the repository under MIT. Existing
fonts/assets retain their original licenses and old source/evidence stay unchanged.

#### Scenario: Continue or reproduce the campaign
- **WHEN** the campaign is inspected
- **THEN** exact sources, inputs, tested outputs, errors and next action are recoverable
- **AND** browser evidence is not relabeled as native, AOD, power, human speed or preference evidence
