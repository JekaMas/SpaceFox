# Historical progress: composition and brighter geography

The 11 November 2026 browser experiment was reported complete and published under MIT. Its single historical task list is [tasks.md](tasks.md); [report](../../../docs/COMPOSITION_RESEARCH.md), [gallery](../../../prototype/composition/review.html) and [summary](evidence/SUMMARY.json) retain the detailed evidence. It explored 18 → 12 → 6 bounded generations. G1 recorded 144 observations and no fully passing variant; G2 recorded 96 and two 8/8 variants; G3 recorded 48 and four 8/8 variants. Three selected compositions retained 24/24 core and 18/18 later observations after parameters were fixed; those later observations were not an independent holdout. Reported map signal outside foreground reservations was 14.36–15.90% of the circle and mean linear Y 0.134–0.364.

The first metric pass produced false failures from CSS padding; v2 used text ranges and reran G1 before parent selection. A later opaque-RGB omission required v3 to recheck ten affected controls across 14 scenes; [correction](evidence/metric-v3-correction.json) and earlier exports remain. Rechecks are not independent samples. Ten exact behavioral cases passed on author source `48b39af98050fce79d6a656342df89fe45e62761`; see [receipt](evidence/verification-final.json) and [retained checks](evidence/retained-checks.json). Historical [publication readback](evidence/publication-readback.json) refers to commit `3d32f5e10c2b34675934068947585314b7f00018`.

The owner subsequently selected M2-31 for [brighter-map refinement](../../../docs/RELAY_REFINEMENT.md); [header comparison](../../../docs/RELAY_HEADER.md) was a separate follow-up. Author visual judgments did not prove child readability, native Garmin, AOD or battery.
