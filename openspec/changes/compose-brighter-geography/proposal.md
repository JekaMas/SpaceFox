# Proposal

## Why

The current marker finalists make the launch point clearer, but geography remains
too dark and whole-face composition was not optimized. The owner requests brighter,
more visible maps and a measured composition-only research campaign.

## What Changes

- Add a separate browser composition laboratory, retaining exact old finalists.
- Fix visibility, luminance, figure-ground, typography and geometry metrics before
  generation one; expose limitations and test misleading counterexamples.
- Run18,12,6 new proposals in three adaptive generations; retain parents, failures,
  source observations and decisions, and compare up to three final proposals.
- Publish original source, reports and specs under MIT within existing authority.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `geography-composition`: measurable browser composition experiments with bright,
  coherent launch geography and preserved watch-face information.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Native watch, update service, catalog and previous marker qualification are
unchanged; new compositions do not inherit the old marker PASS.

## Impact

New `prototype/composition/`, change-owned evidence, focused policy/evidence tests
and an original report. Reuse existing local fonts, geography and rocket catalogs;
do not modify historical renderers or add dependencies, SDKs, services or paid APIs.
