# Design

Garmin's [WatchFace documentation](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/WatchUi/WatchFace.html) and [watch-face guidelines](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/articles/user-experience-guidelines/Watch_Faces.html) describe OS-driven high-power updates and minute low-power updates. Segment34 [uses onUpdate and cached slower fields](https://github.com/ludw/segment34mk3/blob/main/source/Segment34View.mc#L920-L997); Crystal [separates a special live-HR partial update](https://github.com/warmsound/crystal-face/blob/master/source/CrystalView.mc#L275-L305). These are not identical device policies. For FR165, use OS frames and at most one selected-field refresh per minute, with wake/settings and matching callbacks; no new periodic view timer. SFR incremental work and one-shot resume remain independently owned I/O leases.

`NativeDisplayLifecycle` owns frame and field permission, with explicit show/hide/sleep/wake/OFF/ACTIVE/LOW and logical-minute transitions. UTC countdown and values remain separate pure projections; feed and reminders stay with their existing owners. Tests compare production callbacks and effects against an independent logical model, including duplicate and reordered transitions, stopped providers, menu return, expiry and countdown. The combined runner compiles actual Monkey C with pinned SDK/profile and rejects missing selectors, incomplete tables, zero tests and late errors. The hosted CI can exercise its selector manifest and parser controls only unless the Garmin SDK is actually provisioned; it must never label those native execution.
