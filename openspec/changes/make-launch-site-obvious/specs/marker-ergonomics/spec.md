## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three traceable research generations

Research SHALL perform three generations with30,20and10 actual parameter sets,
five exact prior controls, equal five-family budgets and stage-specific primary
research. Hypotheses, parents, failures and decisions SHALL remain traceable.

#### Scenario: Evolve observed hypotheses
- **WHEN** the next generation is prepared
- **THEN** retained preceding findings and new source observations explain its changes
- **AND** unknown parents, duplicate rendered parameters and unmeasured acceptance are rejected

### Requirement: Full launch-marker geometry

The experiment SHALL retain all previous semantic/fit/font/spacing/contrast
requirements and source point identity. Marker core, halo and complete glyph
SHALL use the actual projected point and account for clipping and foreground.

#### Scenario: Measure the complete symbol
- **WHEN** a coordinate exists
- **THEN** the actual full marker has4nativepx clearance from critical foreground and display/geo clipping
- **AND** core/halo/glyph clipping survival is at least98/95/95percent
- **AND** absent coordinates produce no invented marker while site text remains

### Requirement: Honest raster evidence

Diagnostic masks SHALL derive from cloned live marker elements and computed
styles/transforms. Actual native JPEG frames SHALL remain distinct evidence,
with ON/HIDE replicates, unchanged other paint and compression uncertainty.

#### Scenario: Audit actual image support
- **WHEN** a raster claim is made
- **THEN** source-bound masks, original native frames and capture rectangles exist
- **AND** alpha areas are never attributed to JPEG or absent captures
- **AND** native support/contrast exceeds measured repeat noise under the frozen policy

### Requirement: Blind localization and meaning

Independent image-only observers SHALL receive one neutral image per trial,
withheld positions/parameters and no-marker catch trials, and report x/y,
none or unable plus marker meaning before ground truth is released.

#### Scenario: Locate the launch place
- **WHEN** localization is accepted
- **THEN** the answer locates the actual marker within the frozen tolerance and identifies its launch-place meaning
- **AND** no-coordinate/hidden-marker catch trials do not yield false markers
- **AND** model observations are not reported as human speed/preference evidence

### Requirement: Separate screening and fresh confirmation

Source and shortlist SHALL freeze before screen24. Exact final five and source
SHALL freeze before separate unopened confirm24. Failures SHALL not be relabeled
as fresh successes, and raw/summary/scene identities SHALL reconcile.

#### Scenario: Accept a final selection
- **WHEN** final research acceptance is reported
- **THEN** each selected proposal passes the retained known scenes and24fresh scenes under all applicable layers
- **AND** source/candidate/scene drift, contradictions or material visual failures prevent acceptance

### Requirement: Explicit bounds and durable progress

The prior finite RU/EN class SHALL remain unchanged without narrowing product
inputs. Numeric raster floors SHALL be declared heuristics. Objective, progress,
research/tests and publication status SHALL be durably recorded underMIT.

#### Scenario: Inspect the evidence boundary
- **WHEN** results are read or continued
- **THEN** existence, raster support, model localization and human/device evidence are distinguished
- **AND** larger labels/repeated roles remain accepted with outside-class diagnostics
- **AND** an incomplete layer or failed trial is retained as non-success
