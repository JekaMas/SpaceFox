## Question and acceptance regression

The user's observation that the site marker is noticeable mainly in Ticket/Relay is a new acceptance regression. The preceding PASS remains a bounded technical receipt for the old contract; neither geometric presence nor lack of an obvious screenshot overlap established easy localization. Do not defend it as a noticeability result.

The unit for the new deterministic measurements is one frozen candidate × scene × displayed size. The unit for image-only localization is one submitted image and one independent reviewer response. Repeated scenes/styles/reviewers are correlated; counts are corpus coverage, not a human detection rate, causal effect or sample of watch owners.

The new goal is five proposals preserving all old fields and guards, with complete, unoccluded marker paint, supported local contrast in actual images, and no material localization/semantic-identity failure in a preregistered blind image corpus at390,222 and195 pixels. If fewer than five qualify, report the actual smaller count and use a separately recorded repair increment/fresh confirmation; do not promote a failing family to meet a quota.

## Three proof layers

1. **Source and geometry:** the geographic source point, projected anchor, all rendered glyph/core/halo paths with computed styles/transforms, complete stroke/fill extents, watch/geo clipping ancestors, actual opaque foreground containers and required text/icon/rocket gaps. A hardcoded radius8 bounding box is insufficient.
2. **Raster support:** lossless source-bound marker references and actual ON/HIDE images. Reference coverage demonstrates painted-shape support through declared clips/foreground; actual native JPEG corroborates image signal/local contrast, with explicit compression uncertainty. A diagnostic Canvas/reference PNG is never renamed a native screenshot.
3. **Localization/meaning:** a fresh image-only reviewer, given neither coordinate truth nor candidate/style IDs, reports a guessed point or unable and what the mark means. This is an independent qualitative/model-image check, not a timed human visual-search experiment.

Each layer can fail independently. Passing pixel or geometry thresholds cannot rescue a localization failure. A localized high-contrast decorative star is not a correctly identified launch site.

## Preregistered numeric gates

These are explicit engineering heuristics, not empirically validated noticeability cutoffs. They must be accepted/frozen before G1; do not tune them after hidden data or blind answers.

- Preserve all old text/source/value/status/fit/contrast gates and full labels. Marker changes cannot hide or shorten the company, purpose, place, four fields or countdown.
- At390, transparent full-marker reference alpha area at least60px², core at least28px², halo/outline at least30px²; areas scale by(size/390)². Core equivalent diameter at least6 native pixels. These conservative support floors intentionally avoid declaring the user's visible Ticket/Relay controls invisible solely by choosing larger size thresholds. Larger cores8+ and complete glyph extents20+ are hypotheses, not evidence of being noticed.
- At least98% of full core alpha and95% of full glyph/halo alpha survive actual watch/geo clipping and opaque paint. Use the full rendered stroke/fill support, not an anchor point or legacy radius8 proxy. The support/reference rectangle has transparent padding; a reference already clipped at its own image edge cannot establish survival.
- At least4 native pixels of conservative clearance between the complete glyph support and the actual watch/geo clipping boundaries and opaque foreground/required text/icon/rocket paint. Scale this consistently at small display sizes. Actual glyph extents and clips, not recipe dimensions, supply the evidence. Conservative bounding masks may undercount visibility; disclose that limitation rather than silently reducing the margin.
- Actual core contrast against the underlying HIDE counterpart at least3:1 on90% of eligible core pixels; at least50% of the eligible halo/outline pixels have one high-contrast visible outline layer against the local counterpart. Report the whole distributions and eligible-pixel counts. Very small/missing eligible samples are insufficient evidence. Source light/dark outline contrast alone is not contrast against the map beneath it.
- Native JPEG support must exceed a measured replicate noise floor. Use frozen clock/values/fonts, no animation, identical viewport/scroll/face position. Store unmodified originals and exact crop rectangles. Native ON/HIDE marker toggling changes visibility only; non-marker computed paint/layout descriptors must be identical.
- Never require JPEG byte equality. DCT/chroma changes may extend outside the glyph. Use a padded marker ROI of at least16 native pixels; obtain HIDE/HIDE and preferably ON/ON replicates for noise calibration. A changed background/layout or unexplained off-ROI signal invalidates the pair. Signal/core/halo support from JPEG has a declared uncertainty band and is not an exact alpha/coverage measurement.
- Fixed point/geometry/raster failure gates precede any aesthetics/ranking score. A marker fully present but effectively indistinguishable in the actual image still fails the new image-support/localization contract.

A single composite score must not hide a failed field or marker. Report the five old controls against each new criterion, including cases where a conservative proxy and reviewer localization disagree.

## Required raster provenance and counterexamples

A lossless full-glyph reference is cloned from the **live marker subtree**, with computed fill/stroke/opacity/width/dash/transform and inherited sizing inlined. It is rendered on a transparent padded canvas, preserving pixel phase. It is not redrawn from a candidate setting object. Retain the serialized source/reference hash and capture dependency hash. Core and halo/outline semantic subgroups have separate reference masks. A component cannot be claimed fully visible when its own reference is already clipped.

Actual clipping/foreground measurements must include the entire effective chain, including the round watch clip, SVG/geography rectangular clips and opaque backgrounds such as field-row/mission/count-group. Labels/icons/rocket remain protected. If a complex clip cannot be measured, mark coverage unavailable rather than assuming visible.

For native JPEG pairs, archive the raw images, decoded analysis copies, capture dimensions, exact integer crop/scale mapping, source hashes and mode. A decoded/cropped PNG retains JPEG ancestry. Actual195/222 CSS/native views and offline downsampled390 captures are different claims; use actual native small views for the localization package. Diagnostic PNGs and native JPEGs must be visually cross-checked at the same known scenes; preserve any export/glyph discrepancy.

Before trusting the new checker, require these negative examples against an actual healthy record: remove a mandatory component/channel; make a reference touch its own image boundary; put a real opaque container over the full glyph; clip a part of the core/halo at the watch or geo edge; use a tiny core; lower local contrast; shift one ON crop or change non-marker text; fabricate expected marker in a null-coordinate scene; delete raster files or provide NaN/invalid geometry. The healthy baseline must pass. Tests of synthetic fixtures prove checker discrimination only, not product acceptance.

## Blind image-only localization

Use fresh reviewer context(s) without source code, old verdicts, ranks, candidate IDs, coordinates, masks, counterfactuals, debug overlays or truth files. Do not use this technical reviewer as a blinded observer after it has inspected truth. Neutral image IDs are randomized, stripping meaning from filenames/metadata. Retain the private mapping separately. The normal site/country text stays visible because it is part of the product.

Suggested bounded final corpus:60 images =5 finalists ×4 prespecified scenes ×3 sizes. Per finalist include three distinct non-null site scenes and one genuine missing-coordinate scene, with at least one long-label/combined-status scene. Present one exact-size image per trial in randomized order, avoiding adjacent repetitions of the same candidate/scene at different scales. No zoom, crop enhancement, side-by-side counterpart or coordinate hint before the response is locked.

Prompt: “Is there a launch-site marker on this face? If you can locate one, give the point in image pixels (x,y), state what visible sign you used and what it means. If you cannot locate one reliably, answer unable. If none is shown, answer none. Do not infer a point only from the country/place text.” Also record confidence low/medium/high. Do not ask for a reading-time estimate.

After all responses are saved, independently score against the source/raster-bound visible glyph and true anchor. A point inside the actual visible glyph support expanded by6 native pixels, with the correct launch-site interpretation, is localized; a point outside, unable on a non-null scene, a decorative-star interpretation or an invented point on a null scene is a material failure. Preserve raw answers and per-trial outcomes. At195/222 scale the tolerance consistently; do not inflate it to include a large geographic region.

Prefer two separate fresh image-only reviewers for the final corpus. Report them as independent model/reviewer judgments. Their repeated trials are not people and do not yield a human detection probability or p-value. A disagreement is retained for targeted source/image inspection, not resolved by averaging scores until green. The required goal is no remaining material marker localization/identity failure in the agreed final corpus; otherwise cull/repair and use a new independent confirmation if selection changed after confirm disclosure.
