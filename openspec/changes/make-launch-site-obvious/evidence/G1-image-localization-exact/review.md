# Blind image review

Completed all 40 supplied images in order at their original 195 × 195 pixels, viewed individually in batches of five. No zoom, upscale, montage, source inspection, metadata inspection, or truth inspection was used. Answers are locked in answers.json before any truth comparison.

Qualitative observations:

- Teardrop pins and flags communicate a location most directly and supported the strongest launch-site interpretations.
- Yellow-centered circular locators and crosshairs are generally easy to locate, but their launch-site meaning is contextual rather than explicit.
- Very small amber rings are weak at this size, particularly near the right edge or against map detail.
- Cyan diamonds and the tiny blue circular symbol are visible location candidates with ambiguous launch-site semantics; those are classified uncertain.
- Empty map texture was treated as none, without inferring a point from the place text.

This is an image-only qualitative review. No unknown-truth accuracy or timed-human claim is made.
