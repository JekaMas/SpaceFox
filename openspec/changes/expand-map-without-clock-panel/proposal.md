# Proposal

## Why

The owner sees a black block under current time and wants the map larger in both
directions. Replace text-sized map cutouts with glyph contours and broad fades,
then compare half-width, two-thirds and full-width/height compositions.

## What Changes

- Six isolated map hypotheses over the current124px rocket/four-field face.
- No text-sized backing or cutout; thin dark glyph outlines with unchanged fill.
- Broad horizontal/vertical/radial fades, actual source projection and unfaded pin.
- Actual map exposure metrics plus bounded outline diagnostics and visual review.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `wide-map-face`: Broad map backgrounds with outline-protected text and no panels.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Native rendering/AOD remains unimplemented in spacefox-watchface.

## Impact

New sibling prototype/wide-map/ and its evidence only; existing renderers, asset
registry and old green receipts remain unchanged. Update current design/report
after observation. Original source/spec/report MIT; no native or hosting deployment.
