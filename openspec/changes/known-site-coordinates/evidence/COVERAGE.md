This is the provider's pad reference, not independent survey verification or a claim that every Taiyuan pad shares that point. Official provider documentation and its response schema are at https://ll.thespacedevs.com/2.3.0/pads/?format=api . Neither a coordinate's presence nor removal of the visual question mark makes it an independently surveyed point.

The optional per-launch source position is a source fact in the current schedule format, not a separate fixed catalog. Missing or invalid source coordinates and zero/zero do not manufacture a marker. Known fixed catalog positions remain authoritative; mobile reference coordinates alone are not event locations.
