# Publication owner clarification

2026-11-11. The owner confirmed that the official publisher is the adult owner,
using their own Garmin developer account. Exact personal ages are not retained
in public source. This clarifies the publication authority, not Store approval.

Two rules were previously collapsed into an unnecessarily broad implementation
hold. The [developer agreement,II(a)(3)](https://developer.garmin.com/downloads/connect-iq/sdks/agreement.html)
requires an adult developer account/submission. That condition is satisfied by the
owner's clarification. The [review guidelines,1(f)](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/articles/app-review-guidelines/Overview.html)
separately restrict under13-directed apps and qualify the general-audience exception
when under13 use is known. Adult publication alone does not waive that condition.

The earlier spec turned unknown intended-user age into an automatic prerequisite
for any native development. That extrapolation is removed. Implementation can
proceed under the adult owner's authority without requiring a child's age as a
universal gate. Keep truthful audience/intended-use facts and review the applicable
rule in release preparation; do not invent an exemption for private PRG installation
or relabel the product to claim compliance. This is a planning correction, not legal
clearance or a guarantee of Garmin approval. No further private age request is needed
to complete this correction.

Changes are limited to release-lifecycle, the related design/proposal/tasks,
README/runbooks and this correction record. The new adult-publisher scenario and
conditional audience-review scenario replace the blanket hold. The historical
final-review report and53-requirement/99-scenario receipt remain dated evidence;
the current correction has53requirements/100scenarios and its own structural receipt
in publication-owner-checks.json. Old hashes are not rewritten to conceal a contract
change. No rendering, health, update code, assets, service or Store deployment changed.
