## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source-supported rocket appearance
Each accepted current or officially announced launch configuration SHALL resolve to static original model artwork based on the best available source for its visible exterior. Shared artwork SHALL be justified by close exterior similarity or unavailable usable references.

#### Scenario: Known externally distinct models
- **WHEN** reliable sources show distinct exterior geometry for two accepted models
- **THEN** their artwork represents those differences and both resolve offline and through the downloaded-art path.

#### Scenario: Unknown identity or absent reference
- **WHEN** a model identity is unknown or no usable exterior reference exists
- **THEN** the watch uses an explicitly qualified best-effort image without inventing model facts.
