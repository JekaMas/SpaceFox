# Decision and frozen protocol — round 2

Frozen before implementation/selection, 2026-11-11. Objective: three technically qualified, structurally different browser proposals preserving the agreed semantic benchmark; independent reviews must not have unresolved material findings. Human reading time, child preference, native power and physical Garmin fit remain unmeasured. First experiment is not erased.

## Experiment

48 → 24 → 12 = 84 distinct candidates, six initial structural systems, eight initial variants each. Select within systems by hard failure count first, then minimum semantic clearance and geographic visibility. Descendants alter positions, text grammar, map scale or padding and name actual measured parents. At most one repair batch of eight; if still failing, report bounded failure instead of weakening thresholds. Freeze training examples and all hard gates. No training on the sealed independent holdout.

Systems: side-planet mission control; wide atlas/card; low Earthrise with mission-first order; event-first chronology; split instrument; orbital central scene. Some systems may fail due to circle geometry; they still count as tested hypotheses. Final diversity compares clock alignment, fitness grouping/order, mission grammar/anchor and geography anchor; at least two differences per pair, ignoring color/font/projection.

## Measurement

Use actual offscreen 390×390 DOM, document fonts loaded, DOM ranges for each visible line, canvas text metrics for glyph ink. Natural wrapping preserves labels and original full strings. Use a solid opaque dark plate under metadata where geography passes behind it. Text palette and background maximum are declared and checked with sRGB luminance. No stars or gradients beneath critical labels. Minimum 4 px gap applies between measured blocks, between icons/values and between marker halo/foreground. Overlapping same-role wrapped line fragments are not separate blocks. Clock80, site21, metrics22, metadata17+, rocket20+. Missing value uses visible em dash and keeps its configured icon. Unknown point omits marker with no fabricated location.

New rocket art is original schematic SVG: body≥10 px, contrasting outline for dark Electron, two Ariane boosters and explicit generic fallback. These are illustrations, not engineering-accurate geometry or proven model recognition. A short illustrative liftoff uses a user-triggered preview; it does not assert that the real launch occurred.

## Independent evidence

The existing independent reviewer owns the sealed 24-scene input/oracle in a temporary directory. Author receives only schema/class bounds until freeze. It verifies fresh inputs and evidence cross-consistency after freeze. A second new-context reviewer sees only neutral matched-size contact sheets and requirements, without author rankings. This separation reduces expectation leakage; it is not a controlled human study or statistically independent panel.

Fix old guard holes and retain original saved campaign as a positive regression. Old permissive metadata clipping remains historical, not newly declared acceptable.

## Always-on

Render outline time and reduced launch countdown on a real canvas; count every nonblack pixel inside the circular display, including antialiasing. Target <8% leaves margin under Garmin's documented 10% per update. This is representative browser output, not a native firmware, burn-in or battery acceptance test.

## Recorded adaptation and result

Initial source/IDs were frozen before opening the reviewer packet. The fresh
24-scene control failed 1/2/6 scenes across the three proposals. Six repair
variants addressed footer/field/marker geometry. A blind visual finding then
used the last two slots of the same capped eight-candidate generation for a
curved Earth limb and stable paired fitness fields. All eight passed 40 retained
scenes. This is a sequential repair generation, not eight fresh validations.
The original failure, source freezes and every tested set remain available.

Font line rectangles include leading; actual ink bounds are derived from Canvas
fontBoundingBox/actualBoundingBox metrics and retained alongside DOM ranges.
This correction followed a false-positive instrumentation preflight and is not
a human legibility result. A stricter offline detector re-audit kept unchanged
DOM geometry valid while rejecting new independent malformed-record probes.
A misleading fixed north arrow on a rotated map was removed without changing
projection or measured boxes; final-source-binding records the narrow correction.
Final viewport screenshots replace a full-page capture artifact as visual
evidence; historical captures remain with the caveat.
