## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete visible semantics

The qualification laboratory SHALL visibly preserve clock, four configured field roles and values, rocket, operator, purpose, country/site, countdown and independent reschedule/stale indicators. Full source labels SHALL be retained. Accessibility labels alone SHALL not count as visible retention.

#### Scenario: Measure semantic retention
- **WHEN** a benchmark scene is rendered
- **THEN** exact visible labels, line bounds and glyph ink height are measured with no ellipsis, hidden overflow or omitted field
- **AND** a clipped, missing, nonfinite or semantically substituted label fails
- **AND** the benchmark class permits approved localized labels up to 20 graphemes for rocket, 32 each for operator/purpose and 28 for country/site
- **AND** longer real inputs remain accepted with explicit overflow diagnostics, without a full-reading claim

### Requirement: Bounded measured evolution

The experiment SHALL freeze its objective before selection and use measured parents to evolve unique candidates. Rejected candidates and reasons SHALL be retained; scores SHALL not rescue hard failures. Final proposals SHALL differ structurally, with bounded repair and preserved prior failures.

#### Scenario: Evolve measured parents
- **WHEN** a generation is selected
- **THEN** every descendant identifies a measured parent, changed visual parameters and elimination reasons
- **AND** duplicate parameters, unknown parents or incomplete measurements prevent acceptance
- **AND** generations contain 48 initial candidates from at least six structural systems, then 24 and 12 descendants
- **AND** three final proposals differ in at least two layout dimensions excluding color, font and projection alone
- **AND** at most one eight-candidate repair generation is allowed, with prior failures retained

### Requirement: Qualified measurements and evidence

The detector SHALL reject malformed geometry, missing or unexpected scene identities, contradictory summaries and incomplete observations. It SHALL check exact benchmark text, circular fit, critical spacing, hierarchy and contrast against declared worst-case backgrounds. Contrast and ink measurements SHALL be reported as browser proxies, not human legibility or native certification.

#### Scenario: Reject plausible bad evidence
- **WHEN** NaN geometry, unrelated scene IDs, a contradictory failed audit, an omitted role or a near collision is supplied
- **THEN** qualification fails while literal independently specified healthy examples pass
- **AND** critical blocks require at least 4 native pixels of separation, text contrast at least 4.5:1 and essential graphics at least 3:1
- **AND** clock font is 80 native pixels, metric values at least 22, site at least 21, metadata at least 17 and rocket label at least 20
- **AND** rocket schematic bodies are at least 10 pixels wide

### Requirement: Independent final comparison

The author SHALL freeze candidate IDs and source digests before reading a reviewer-owned 24-scene holdout. Failures SHALL be retained and fresh testing distinguished from regression after repair. A reviewer without authorship SHALL inspect actual evidence; a separate blind reviewer SHALL inspect neutral images. Three justified proposals SHALL state uncertainty and scope limits.

#### Scenario: Qualify frozen finalists
- **WHEN** final results are reported
- **THEN** each proposal has complete exact holdout observations and independent technical and blind visual review
- **AND** absent observations, contradictory evidence or unresolved material findings prevent a claim of qualification
- **AND** holdout scenes vary RU/EN, status flags, day-scale countdowns, configured fitness roles, missing values and points beyond the original three
- **AND** blind images have matched sizes and no rankings or author recommendations

### Requirement: Sparse always-on preview

Each proposal SHALL include a 390-pixel sparse always-on sketch with measured nonblack pixels within the round screen and a project target below 8 percent. Reports SHALL distinguish this conservative representative-frame measurement from Garmin's 10 percent rule, burn-in behavior, battery testing and native rendering.

#### Scenario: Count sparse pixels
- **WHEN** a sparse preview is rendered
- **THEN** actual canvas pixel counts are retained and a deliberately overfilled frame fails the project target
