# Why

Independent review of the 319-layout exploration found real semantic clipping, weak qualification guards and three insufficiently distinct compositions. The old geometric results remain valid within their old contract; they do not establish a readable final watch face.

# What Changes

- Run a bounded second experiment: 48 initial candidates across six composition systems, 24 measured descendants and 12 measured descendants; at most eight repairs after a retained failure.
- Require visible, complete approved labels, controlled spacing/contrast, larger original rocket schematics and three distinct information layouts.
- Freeze finalists before opening a reviewer-owned 24-scene holdout. Obtain a separate blind visual review and retain dissent.
- Repair malformed-evidence acceptance in the original detector without rewriting historical results.
- Publish three justified browser proposals, sparse always-on sketches and reproducible evidence under the repository MIT license.

# Capabilities

## New Capabilities
- `layout-qualification`: semantic, visual and evidence qualification of candidate watch-face layouts.

## Modified Capabilities
None. The earlier change remains historical and the native application remains unimplemented.

# Impact

New browser laboratory, focused detector repairs, tests, specifications and research evidence. Reuses local fonts, original artwork and geographic data. No new packages, paid services, SDK installation or native release.
