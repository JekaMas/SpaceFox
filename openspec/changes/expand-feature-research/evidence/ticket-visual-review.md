## Circle-edge impression and hierarchy

All four are centered mission-first layouts: status signs, rocket/company, purpose, four values, then a large clock between rocket and globe when geography is available, followed by location and countdown/unknown-time text.

The company and purpose remain within the upper arc. Long operator text is wrapped rather than visibly cut by the circle. The two-line purpose in scene2 makes the upper block denser, but its last line remains separate from the numeric row. The large time is the strongest single graphic, while the mission is the first block encountered from the top. This is coherent for a launch-following watch face.

The long scene1 location `Новая Зеландия · Тест Купол` has a closer relationship to the lower side edges than the shorter labels, but its visible beginning and ending stay inside the circle. No clipped edge impression rises to a material problem in the provided viewport. A larger safe margin cannot be certified from these samples.

The globe is close to the right arc in scenes0/1/3 but its circular shape remains distinguishable, and the marker is visible inside it. It does not collide with time digits, mission text, or the location line. The rocket remains separate on the other side of the clock.

In scene2 the empty area to the right of the clock is visibly quieter because the geographic object is absent. Under the clarified missing-coordinate contract this is honest missing-data behavior. It should not be described as a screenshot proving a map or marker for that scene.
