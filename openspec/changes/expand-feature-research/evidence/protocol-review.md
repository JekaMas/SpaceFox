# Independent protocol review — feature research round 3

The proposed next cycle is suitable for a bounded design search after the clarifications accepted by the author. This is not an independent verdict on yet-unrendered candidates. I own only reviewer artifacts under `/tmp/spacefox-feature-round3-review`; I do not author or edit the production implementation.

## Accepted objective and accounting

Select five proposals jointly from new candidates and the three preceding controls. All five must pass the same 32 author scenes, the 24 reviewer screening scenes, and a separately unopened 24-scene confirmation set. At least two selected reading structures must be new relative to the preceding three. A new structure changes at least two of grouping, reading order, mission anchor, and clock anchor. A color, typeface, geographic projection or renamed ID does not supply structural novelty.

The accepted initial budget is 12 axes × (8 non-base levels + 4 descendants) = 144 axis candidates, then 64 + 32 + 16 = 112 joint candidates, plus three exact preceding controls and one common anchor: **260 initial unique candidates**. A shared base is counted once, not once per axis. Four axis descendants must actually change that axis's rendering parameter, retain a measured parent, and remain distinguishable from every other rendering parameter set. Joint candidates retain their measured parent/feature provenance. Count actual visually active parameter signatures, including normalization rules, rather than treating a changed ID/description as a new experiment. Preserve failed observations and rejection reasons.

Freeze a shortlist of **12** and its source/parameter bindings before opening screen24. At most **12 repair candidates** may follow retained failures, before the top-five freeze; retain their first-attempt failures and describe reuse of screening data as regression. The final five, source digests, labels, fit/typography rules and selection procedure must be frozen before opening confirm24. Confirm24 is evaluated once against those five. A confirmation failure prevents a completed/qualified top-five claim; it cannot be repaired, replaced or reselected against that same set. A further cycle would require a new separately owned unopened confirmation set, rather than relabeling this set as fresh.

Relay and ticket structures are useful explicit hypotheses, but neither is an automatic winner. Selection must depend on retained measurements and independent image critique. If the fixed budget cannot produce the required two new viable structures and five passing proposals, report the bounded failure instead of weakening structural or fit criteria.

## Comparable typography and frozen hard gates

Normalize the clock on the same literal glyph probe `08:08` to **58.5 ± 2 native pixels of actual glyph height** before candidate selection. Retain the measured probe, actual font family/weight, font size and any tracking. Each actual scene's clock ink height must be at least **55 px**; equal CSS font size alone is not comparable across typefaces. Apply this as a new round-three protocol, without rewriting prior receipts or claiming that old font-size checks prove the new criterion. Render the three old controls in the same new benchmark because scene inputs and typography assertions have changed.

Retain exact visible clock, four configured field roles and values, rocket name, approved operator/purpose/site labels and countdown, with independent reschedule and stale indicators. No ellipsis, hidden overflow, aria-only substitutes, invented fallback health values or fabricated coordinates may count as a pass. Zero countdown does not assert liftoff. Unknown time and missing coordinates remain separate states.

Hard gates are finite/nonempty measurements; true circular glyph/essential-graphic fit; at least **4 px** between critical blocks, icon/value pairs and marker halo/foreground; text contrast at least **4.5:1** and essential graphics at least **3:1** against an inspectable worst-case background; metadata font size ≥17, site ≥21, fitness values ≥22 and rocket label ≥20 native pixels; known countdown font ≥31 and unknown-time label ≥16. Keep the prior ≥10 px schematic body-width floor unless the new protocol explicitly replaces it before implementation. Clock ink normalization does not waive the other labels' minimum sizes or complete semantics. Store their actual ink heights too, without mislabeling em size as ink height.

Continue to reject nonfinite marker contrast, empty glyph-line records, contradictory physical badge/summary flags, missing/duplicate/unexpected scene IDs, absent configured roles and contradictions between raw observations and summary qualification. Exact raw source-data/display-label comparisons are needed; a detector's own green summary is not an independent oracle.

Use one shared map projection for land/grid and marker. Geographic orientation cues must be truthful for the actual projection; the prior fixed-arrow error must not return. Preserve explicit generic rocket fallback and model/asset identity. Do not infer engineering accuracy or model recognition from schematic contrast/body width. Any feature that introduces changing visible geometry needs an explicitly declared measured state boundary; resting-frame measurements must not be advertised as animation-frame qualification.

## Independent sets and release procedure

I will prepare two separate sealed packets, screen24 and confirm24, using the accepted round-two schema. Each has twelve distinct payloads localized to RU/EN. Country names are real; locations, point selections, missions, names and fitness values are explicitly synthetic test material, with country membership checked against the repository's existing Natural Earth polygons. No actual launch schedule, private telemetry, paid service or new external account is used.

Only the interface and class bounds are available to the implementation author before release. Inputs, scene identities, generator details, oracle and hashes remain private. Screen is released only after the shortlist/source freeze. Confirm is not released or previewed until the top-five/source freeze. Sources and candidate parameters are checked at each release. The author must not infer the confirmation set from screening copies or silently replace either packet. Published previous controls are regression material, not fresh holdouts.

The accepted fully readable display class is rocket ≤20 graphemes, operator and purpose ≤32, site display label ≤28; supplied approved labels are preserved in full, with full source originals retained. The numeric/display class stated in `schema.md` was explicitly accepted by the author before sealing. Repeated roles and larger values remain valid product inputs with outside-benchmark diagnostics; this benchmark does not narrow product settings. Longer real inputs remain accepted with explicit overflow diagnostics, but are outside the full-reading claim. RU/EN comparability does not prove other locale coverage.

## Slot-mapping clarification before screening

The first reviewer schema added a universal row-major reading-order assumption. Before screening/source freeze, the author clarified the actual user contract: logical configurable roles and values remain stable while column-major/other declared reading routes are allowed feature hypotheses. I accept this correction. Payloads and oracle slot identities are unchanged; the spatial audit will validate each frozen declared mapping rather than impose DOM or row-major order. The initial seals/files are retained, and the metadata amendment is explicit in slot-mapping-clarification.md.

## Evidence and interpretation

The reviewer will write a separate raw-observation audit implementation after observations arrive. The implementation renderer/evaluator is not that oracle. Retain exact scene coverage, per-candidate failures, source and font bindings, ancestry, source-effect invalidation notes, and the first screening/confirmation results. Reuse prior valid greens when their semantic input is unchanged; re-evaluate only changed owners/assertions/fixtures. A re-audit of existing observations must not be called a fresh render or fresh holdout.

Use neutral matched-size viewport images and a visual reviewer outside implementation authorship, without author ranks or preferred candidates. Preserve critique and disposition; after feedback, further inspection is informed regression. Bind final presentation data and actual image-capture method as well as the measured renderer. Earlier full-page capture artifacts showed why the image itself needs source/viewport cross-checking. Native-pixel PNGs, not JPEG screenshots, are the correct source for exact AOD pixel counts if that budget remains part of the claim.

Reports may say that a candidate passes a specified finite synthetic benchmark or that a reviewer prefers its visual coherence. They must not infer human reading speed, child preference, model-recognition accuracy, physical Garmin legibility, live-feed reliability, native font support or battery use. Twelve localized payload pairs are not 24 independent real missions or human participants; no confidence intervals, p-values or user-success rates follow from these selected stress cases. Geographic window area is not geographic comprehension. A bounded parameter search with independent control is useful engineering evidence without pretending to be a human experiment.
