# Independent reconfirm24 protocol

This is a newly authored and separately sealed24-scene confirmation set for an explicit increment after the retained failed first confirmation. It was prepared and sealed before the proposed production repair or candidate selection: the four core/renderer hashes still match the first confirmation freeze at sealing. No values, sceneIDs, generator details or hashes are disclosed to the implementation author before the new second candidate/source freeze.

It has twelve new synthetic payloads localized toRU/EN, all eight combinations of the independent time-known/rescheduled/stale flags, new country/site/point combinations, long day durations, wide configured fields and unavailable values/coordinates. Countries are real and points are checked inside the existing Natural Earth polygons; these synthetic locations are not claimed actual launch pads. Vehicle/company/mission combinations are synthetic, with registered known schematics or explicit generic fallback. The [Natural Earth primary description](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/) identifies the map-source family. Local polygon containment is not a surveyed-border or operational launch claim.

The accepted class and slot-mapping semantics remain those in schema.md. Exact approved display strings and logical field identities are preserved. Independent observers must validate declared logical-slot positions. No threshold is weakened to accommodate the previous failures. A better diagnostic of invisible trailing whitespace may be tested as a new measurement owner, while preserving every non-whitespace codepoint and the complete source string.

The previous screen/confirm sets are known and are regression material after repair. This set is not used for repair or selection. After its second freeze release, evaluate the frozen final candidates once, retain all outcomes, and do not replace/tune a candidate against this set. A failure remains a failure and would require a separately declared further increment with another unopened set.

Do not rerun build_packet.py: IDs are minted once. source.json and generation details belong to this packet alone. PRIVATE-prepare-reconfirm.py remains private until this release and is not needed to use the packet. No secret product/telemetry data, paid service, external account, native execution or human experiment is involved. All newly authored packet material is MIT for authorized post-release publication.
