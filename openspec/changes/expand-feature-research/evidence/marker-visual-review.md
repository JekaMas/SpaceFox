## Aesthetic preference

**Weak preference: B, the Earth on the right.** It keeps the mission text and launch location against a quieter black region, while the Earth forms a separate right-hand graphic layer beside the rocket. This supports the relatively calm time-first, two-column-values route.

**A is also visually valid.** It shows more of the Earth across the left/lower circle and gives the design a stronger geographic atmosphere. The cost is more visible grid and land underneath/around the text panels, making the black strips more apparent. That can be a worthwhile choice if geographical presence is preferred over quietness.

A/B are alternatives within the same reading route, not two new structural concepts. The preference is small, editorial, and limited to the shown geometry. It is not evidence of reading speed, error reduction, battery use, or a child's preference. No comparison to an unshown current geometry is claimed.

## Bounded conclusion and limits

For these actual repair images, the required point/halo is visible in both candidates, including all mandatory-coordinate scenes0–3. Text, four values, rocket, clock, site, and countdown/unknown-time roles remain visibly intact. Either candidate can be retained on visual grounds; B is my modest stylistic preference.

The review does not establish physical Garmin readability, sunlight/motion behavior, font rasterization, exact text equality with a source, all possible inputs, coordinate accuracy, user preference, AOD acceptance, animation, performance, battery, or technical confirmation. The small dark AOD previews are insufficient for AOD approval.
