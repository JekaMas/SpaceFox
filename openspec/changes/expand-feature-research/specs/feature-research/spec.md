## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Traceable feature matrix

The research SHALL record twelve independently named mutable axes, their anchor, eight non-anchor levels, source-derived hypotheses, expected observations and repair boundaries. Reference observations SHALL be distinguished from empirical findings and from author inference.

#### Scenario: Inspect feature hypotheses
- **WHEN** the matrix is inspected
- **THEN** each axis identifies actual rendered parameters and at least two source-informed hypotheses
- **AND** unseen references or unperformed human tests are not reported as observations

### Requirement: Feature and joint generations

The laboratory SHALL measure feature-isolated generations before joint combinations, preserve exact parents and deduplicate actual visual parameters. Hard failures SHALL not be rescued by ranking. Previous results SHALL be re-rendered in the common benchmark.

#### Scenario: Evolve feature evidence
- **WHEN** measured results are evolved
- **THEN** each of12 axes has8 initial non-anchor variants and4 descendants, followed by64/32/16 joint variants
- **AND** one shared anchor and three exact previous controls give260 initial sets
- **AND** empty measurements, unknown parents, duplicate visuals or changes outside the declared isolated axis are rejected

### Requirement: Honest rendered measurements

Measurements SHALL preserve exact approved labels, four configured roles, status semantics and projected site identity. They SHALL check finite geometry, circular fit, spacing, contrast, type hierarchy and visible geography from actual browser observations. Scores SHALL remain proxies with explicit limits.

#### Scenario: Measure constraints
- **WHEN** a scene is rendered
- **THEN** clock probe08:08 is normalized to58.5±2px ink and actual clock ink is at least55px
- **AND** fonts remain at least22px for fitness,21site,20rocket,17metadata,31known-countdown or16unknown
- **AND** critical block separation is at least4px, text contrast4.5:1 and essential graphic contrast3:1
- **AND** clipped/substituted labels, missing physical indicators, nonfinite data and hidden/occluded markers fail
- **AND** viewport area and visible land are separate proxies, with zero geography when coordinates are absent

### Requirement: Combined top five confirmation

Selection SHALL combine new results with exact preceding controls, freeze a shortlist of12 before reviewer screening24, and permit at most12 repairs before top-five freeze. A separate unopened confirmation24 and independent technical and neutral-image reviews SHALL assess the frozen final five.

#### Scenario: Confirm the selection
- **WHEN** completion is claimed
- **THEN** all5 pass32author,24screen and24unseen-confirmation scenes without post-confirmation tuning
- **AND** at least2 selected structures change at least2 of grouping, reading order, mission anchor and clock anchor relative to previous structures
- **AND** missing scene identities, contradictory summaries or material unresolved review findings prevent acceptance
- **AND** a failed confirmation requires an explicitly separate retained increment, not relabeling regression as fresh evidence

### Requirement: Explicit benchmark boundaries

The benchmark SHALL define its input class without narrowing accepted product settings. Full source labels SHALL be retained. Out-of-class inputs SHALL remain accepted with diagnostics. Results SHALL distinguish browser checks, human preference, native behavior and power evidence.

#### Scenario: Retain difficult inputs
- **WHEN** inputs exceed the finite qualification class
- **THEN** they remain renderable and outside-class diagnostics prevent an unsupported full-reading claim
- **AND** the class usesRU/EN, validHH:MM, labels up to20rocket/32operator/32purpose/28site/16countdown graphemes and4distinct fitness roles with displays up to7graphemes
- **AND** repeated field roles are accepted settings outside this finite benchmark
- **AND** sparse390px canvas frames require actual pixel counts below8percent and do not establish native battery or burn-in behavior
