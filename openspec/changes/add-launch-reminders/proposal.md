# Proposal

## Why

Launch reminders would help the wearer follow an upcoming launch without watching the countdown continuously. The requested vibration cadence and quiet behavior must work when the watch face is not actively drawing.

## What Changes

- Preserve the requested reminder behavior as an unimplemented capability while recording the Garmin feasibility investigation.
- Do not add a menu option, permission, binary, or reduced notification contract until a viable architecture and its acceptance evidence exist.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `launch-reminders`: opt-in, quiet-aware vibration cues for a known upcoming launch.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This change currently adds only an unimplemented specification and evidence. A future implementation affects watch settings, launch selection, scheduling and quiet-state handling and requires a separately established viable execution path. No phone service, activity session, device or firmware change is authorized by this record.
