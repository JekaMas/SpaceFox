# Testing SpaceFox 0.1.2 candidate

This candidate targets the watches listed in [supported devices](docs/SUPPORTED_DEVICES.md), including **Garmin Forerunner 165 Music**. The signed IQ is intended for Garmin's Connect IQ distribution process. A standalone PRG is supplied for a USB test where the device permits it; installation on an actual FR165 has not yet been observed. Do not assume that tapping the IQ or PRG on a phone installs it.

[SpaceFox on Garmin Connect IQ](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/7ef095e4-72fe-44cd-bc39-8987b6ab49e3) version 0.1.2 has been submitted and is **pending review**. The owner selected Forerunner 165 Music through Download / Add to Device, accepted the requested permissions and received a confirmation that SpaceFox will be installed at the next device sync. For that already queued watch, open Garmin Connect and sync, then choose SpaceFox from the watch face menu and apply it. Queue confirmation does not establish that the installation completed. Garmin says the preview is visible only to the submitting account until approval. For the owner preview, sign in to the mobile Connect IQ app with that same Garmin account, select the paired watch and follow the app's installation steps if the preview is available there. The web page directs installation to the mobile app; the actual phone installation has not been observed. A separate Garmin account must wait for approval or use a supported USB installation. A GitHub prerelease is not Garmin approval.

For a USB test, connect the watch to a computer, copy the PRG for the exact model, such as `SpaceFox-fr165m.prg` for Forerunner 165 Music, into the watch's `GARMIN/APPS` directory when the device exposes it, safely eject, then select SpaceFox on the watch. File-system access and installation vary by device and computer and have not been verified on a physical watch. Keep the previous version in case recovery requires it; settings survival and downgrade are not promised.

A clean install defaults to English; Field 1 steps, Field 2 Body Battery, Field 3 heart rate and Field 4 stress; mint clock and countdown; white field values; and `https://jekamas.github.io/SpaceFox` as the feed URL. The watch needs its normal Garmin communications connection to fetch the schedule; the host must publish a compatible schema-3 SFR1M scene. The public feed passed a portable compatibility check on 14 November 2026; first background synchronization and its timing have not been observed on a watch. The clock and last accepted data can work offline, subject to the device and cache state. This build does not expose a settings view on the watch; availability of phone settings has not been verified. The developer simulator is not evidence of physical watch, phone, battery or Store behavior.

Check the clock and four fields, wait for a schedule and rocket/map, and compare the launch against its source. Confirm `T-` before a known target and `T+` after it if that situation is encountered; zero alone is not confirmation of liftoff. Try offline display, reconnection and reboot. Note the watch firmware, app version, phone model, approximate time and steps for any problem. Report at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/JekaMas/SpaceFox/issues), omitting health readings or other private details from screenshots.
