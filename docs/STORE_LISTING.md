# SpaceFox 0.1.2 test listing

SpaceFox is a space-themed watch face with a 24-hour clock, four configurable activity fields, and the next available launch with rocket artwork and a launch-site map. The countdown shows T- before a known launch time and T+ afterwards; reaching zero does not confirm liftoff. Rescheduled and stale information have separate indicators.

Choose from English, Russian, German, French and Spanish, configure the four fields and colors, and use the default public launch feed or a compatible feed of your own. Available activity readings depend on your watch. A connection through Garmin Connect is needed to update launch data; the last accepted data can remain available offline.

Version 0.1.2 targets Forerunner 165 and 165 Music, Forerunner 170 and 170 Music, Forerunner 570 42mm, Forerunner 70, Venu 3S, Venu 4 41mm and vívoactive 6. This is a test release; installation and behavior on physical watches have not yet been verified. Report problems at https://github.com/JekaMas/SpaceFox/issues.
