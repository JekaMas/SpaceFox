# Historical global catalog audit

The [current visual catalog](GLOBAL_VISUAL_CATALOG.md), [design](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/design.md) and [requirements](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/specs/visual-catalog/spec.md) own current coverage and commands. This document refers to the 11 November 2026 source snapshot; it is not a current global completeness claim. Country of launch site, rocket origin, manufacturer and operator are separate identities. Valid-looking coordinates do not prove exact site location; incomplete metadata and missing model-specific artwork must remain visible as uncertainty. MIT rights in original project materials do not transfer rights in third-party data, images or marks.

The [named historical inventory](GLOBAL_CATALOG_INVENTORY.md) and [reference data](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/evidence/global-catalog/reference-inventory.json) retain the original source slice.
