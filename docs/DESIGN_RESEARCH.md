# Historical browser study: geography and typography

The [gallery](../prototype/layouts.html) compared eight active-screen alternatives using the same demonstration values, four fields, rocket, company, mission, countdown and site. It was not a final native layout or a physical FR165 test. Coordinates and marker projection were kept consistent. A map or globe height of one quarter, one third or one half meant roughly 98, 130 or 195 px of a 390 px diameter, not the fraction of the circular area occupied by land.

The qualitative comparison proposed H and C among globe arrangements and E among flat maps for further exploration. These were visual recommendations without measured reading times or user tests; the subsequently selected current design is documented separately in the [watch-face change](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/design.md).

The study compared Roboto Condensed 600, Barlow Semi Condensed 600, Rajdhani 600 and Space Grotesk 600 for numbers, with Noto Sans 500 labels. Font provenance and licenses are in `prototype/fonts/manifest.json`; font assets retain their own SIL OFL 1.1 terms. Browser Latin/Cyrillic subsets do not qualify all 36 languages on the watch. Native conversion, memory, glyph/RTL and AOD limits require their own evidence. A filled active-screen map or globe cannot be assumed suitable for AOD.

The report used typography and platform guidance as design inputs, not Garmin-specific usability evidence or proof of readability for a child. This English condensation is not a rerun.
