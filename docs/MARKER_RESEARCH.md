# Historical launch-marker research

The 11 November 2026 browser research addressed a gap between having a geometrically present marker and being able to locate it and identify its meaning in an image. It used source/geometry checks, rendered image signal and a separate image-only identification exercise; the latter was model review, not a human experiment. The project thresholds were engineering heuristics rather than Garmin or human-vision standards. [Progress](../openspec/changes/make-launch-site-obvious/PROGRESS.md), [specification](../openspec/changes/make-launch-site-obvious/specs/marker-ergonomics/spec.md), [results](../openspec/changes/make-launch-site-obvious/evidence/RESULTS.json) and [replay](../openspec/changes/make-launch-site-obvious/evidence/REPLAY.md) preserve the generations, independent observations, uncertainty and failures.

Five finalists passed the bounded marker protocol; a later [whole-face comparison](FINALISTS_COMPARISON.md) did not prove overall ergonomic superiority. The current fixed flat-map requirement is in the [watch-face design](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/design.md).
