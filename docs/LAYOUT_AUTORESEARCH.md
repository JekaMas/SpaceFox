# Historical layout research

The first browser study planned at least 200 checked layouts and three distinct finalists, with 192 + 48 + 24 planned variants and predetermined size, geometry and control thresholds. Its results used the original contract. A later independent review found clipping that the old contract allowed and strengthened the checks; the [qualification report](LAYOUT_QUALIFICATION.md) retains its findings, failures and later proposals. These browser measurements do not establish human reading speed, a child's preferences or Garmin-device operation.

The English condensation is not a rerun. The complete initial protocol, metrics, results and limitations are available as the [previous public report](https://github.com/JekaMas/SpaceFox/blob/59f16c43b453db0573a43d5ebec98a0b019a08fd/docs/LAYOUT_AUTORESEARCH.md). Current product choices are in the [watch-face design](../openspec/changes/spacefox-watchface/design.md).

The [previous public document](https://github.com/JekaMas/SpaceFox/blob/59f16c43b453db0573a43d5ebec98a0b019a08fd/docs/LAYOUT_AUTORESEARCH.md) retains the original dated research and source links. Historical observations retain their original evidence boundaries.
