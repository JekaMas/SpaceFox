# SpaceFox 0.1.13 candidate testing

This is a local signed candidate, pending package verification and publication. For a future USB test, select the PRG matching the Forerunner 165 or 165 Music and follow [installation instructions](INSTALLATION.md). In watch-face settings, check that the running version is 0.1.13. The settings header has not yet been observed on the signed simulator watch face or physical watch.

Check the selected launch, rocket, site marker and active second countdown. In always-on mode check the large clock, date, weekday, battery, map outline and site point; the countdown has minute precision and the four custom fields are hidden. Rescheduled launches use a reddish countdown. A zero countdown does not establish liftoff. Recheck an ordinary sleep-to-active transition and record the watch model, firmware, displayed version and elapsed wake delay. Keep private activity values and credentials out of reports.

A retained public launch cache was used for the signed Music simulator observation. It showed the decoded rocket and flag, full always-on outline and point, and the retained scene after a manually triggered temporal event. That event's network or new-generation disposition was not observed. Simulator values were simulated and do not establish physical wake latency, installation, power use, phone delivery or Store approval.
