# SpaceFox 0.1.14 test guide

Use the PRG matching your Forerunner 165 or 165 Music and follow [USB installation](INSTALLATION-0.1.14.md). Check that watch-face settings show version 0.1.14. The IQ export is not a phone installer.

Check the selected launch, unmarked rocket, country flag, site marker, four fields and active seconds. In always-on mode check the large clock, date, weekday, battery, map outline and site point; the countdown has minute precision and the four custom fields are hidden. A reschedule uses a reddish countdown. At the scheduled time the countdown becomes T+; zero does not confirm liftoff.

Try a normal sleep-to-active transition and record the watch model, firmware, displayed version and wake delay. A paired phone supplies Garmin's normal data connection; physical first sync and timing remain unverified. Keep health readings and private activity data out of reports.

A 0.1.13 source SDK 9.3 ordinary-memory diagnostic using a controlled Cape fixture reopened its installed program without push and completed the decoded map, rocket and flag scene in 5,703 ms; this is one test-key, instrumented simulator observation, not an observation of this signed program or a physical timing guarantee. An earlier four-phase simulator LOW observation found no continuously lit pixels in its measured interior; its outer five-pixel simulator bezel was excluded. This is not a physical burn-in or full-map guarantee.

Earlier 0.1.13 SDK 9.3 EN/RU captures of the corrected settings header and warning used a separate test-key watch-app host with an injected rejection; system settings entry on this signed program and physical watch remain unobserved. Physical wake, battery life, phone delivery and Store approval remain open.
