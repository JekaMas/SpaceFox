# SpaceFox 0.1.12 test installation

Choose `SpaceFox-fr165.prg` for Forerunner 165 or `SpaceFox-fr165m.prg` for Forerunner 165 Music. These are signed test builds, not a Garmin Store release. Use a computer and USB data cable: connect the watch, open its Garmin storage (or MTP device), and copy the matching PRG into `GARMIN/APPS`. Safely eject the watch, disconnect it, and select SpaceFox in the watch-face menu. MTP transfer may require a compatible file-transfer application on some computers. Do not tap a PRG or IQ file on the phone expecting Garmin Connect to install it. The IQ package is an SDK export for inspection and future Garmin submission, not a phone installer.

SpaceFox uses the paired phone's normal Garmin communications connection to retrieve its public launch feed. The clock and previously accepted data can remain visible offline. First sync and its timing on a physical watch have not been verified. In watch-face settings, confirm the version is 0.1.12; the version is not shown on the face. Do not remove existing app data as a first troubleshooting step.

Check the clock, four fields, launch name, rocket, map point and countdown. The countdown switches from T- to T+ at the scheduled time; zero does not confirm a launch. Try a normal disconnect and reconnect and note the model, firmware, settings version, approximate time and steps if something is wrong. Avoid including health readings or private activity data in screenshots. Simulator tests cannot establish physical installation, Bluetooth delivery, sensors, battery life or Store acceptance.
