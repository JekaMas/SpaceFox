# SpaceFox private beta

The owner-only Garmin beta uses `private-beta.json` as its persistent application identity and Store title, SpaceFox Beta. It is a separate app from production, so settings and update identity are separate. The on-watch name remains SpaceFox. The historical local 0.1.4 candidate used nine device profiles. Its previous published beta is 0.1.3; the candidate is not evidence of an upload or installation.

The observed [SpaceFox Beta 0.1.3 listing](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/db2b9b57-0d38-4df5-ae04-8cfabc0b81c9) is owner-only. Sign in to Connect IQ on the phone with the same Garmin account that submitted the beta; another account cannot download this owner-only listing. The beta is a separate app identity from the ordinary SpaceFox listing and has separate settings and updates.

The owner selected the paired Forerunner 165 Music, accepted the requested permissions, and Garmin confirmed the beta is queued for the next device sync. Open Garmin Connect on the phone and sync the watch; once installation completes, select SpaceFox from the watch face menu. The queue confirmation does not establish that phone sync or physical installation completed.

The former 0.1.4/nine-profile beta builder and its operator recipe were retired
in performance021. The records above describe the original submission and queue;
they are historical observations and do not provide a current build route. Use
[the current release guide](../docs/RELEASE_AND_UPDATES.md) for current local
release preparation. Retirement does not change either published app identity.
