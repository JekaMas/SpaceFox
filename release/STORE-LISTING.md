## Full description

SpaceFox shows a rocket, a launch-site map and country flag, mission details, time and a locally calculated T−/T+ countdown. Choose four fields from steps, Body Battery, heart rate, stress, calories and watch battery. Launch estimates can change; a countdown reaching zero does not confirm liftoff. Missing Garmin values remain unavailable.

The feed URL defaults to `https://jekamas.github.io/SpaceFox`; a different compatible HTTPS feed can be configured where settings are available. Keep Garmin Connect connected for background updates. Schedule and imagery may take several background events to arrive. The current hosted feed must be verified compatible before release.

This is an experimental test build. Retained observations are from the official Garmin simulator; physical watch and phone operation and battery use remain unverified. SpaceFox is free and open source, without a SpaceFox account, advertising or paid fields. Health values stay on the watch; network requests fetch public launch data and artwork. The map identifies launch sites, not your location.

SpaceFox is independent and is not endorsed by Garmin or launch operators. Launch schedules are informational and can change. Source data: The Space Devs / Launch Library 2. Third-party notices are retained in the repository.
